**Approval rule (operator, 10-24): every public post on a new channel is Tier 2 until 2026-11-07.**
Drafts go to `reports/funnel/social/drafts/YYYY-MM-DD/` (caption, asset, target account, creator
credits) with one operator-queue row per batch; nothing posts until the operator replies
"approve social <date>". After 11-07 Quinn may propose moving it to Tier 1.

**Voice rule (operator, 10-24): "it can't look like AI wrote it."** For every caption, script and
post: write like a Simmer talking to Simmers — short, specific, a little opinionated; name the mod
and the creator; one concrete detail you can see in the image. Never: "elevate", "dive in",
"game-changer", "must-see", "unleash", "whether you're… or…", em-dash chains, triple-adjective
lists, rhetorical-question openers, emoji walls, hashtag stacks (≤3, real community tags). Read it
aloud; if it sounds like an ad, rewrite it. Video voice-over: none or human; text-on-screen only
otherwise. No AI-generated faces or fake reactions.

**Creator assets:** every video/post credits the creator on screen and links their page; keep an
opt-out list (`creator-optout`) and honour it before any asset is cut. NSFW mods never appear.

**Until the operator's accounts and tokens land:** build the pipeline (asset generator from the
catalog, caption drafts, posting scripts behind a disabled flag) and put the first week's drafts in
the queue, so posting starts the day access arrives.

## Paid ads test (budget pending — operator to-do)
At ≈$16.50 per 1,000 sessions a paid session loses money; only a subscriber or returning user pays
back. Test design (Rio + Cass): one EA pack launch, $100–200, Reddit or TikTok, graded on cost per
email subscriber and 14-day return rate, not CPC. No bids on competitor trademarks ("CurseForge").
Positioning vs CurseForge/SimsFinds: "search every site at once."

## Run — creators and the community vote
- **Validated creator accounts** (Nova): verified email + proof of ownership (link back from the
  creator's Patreon/Tumblr/CurseForge page) before a listing goes live; protects against scammers
  impersonating creators or hijacking listings.
- **"Featured on MustHaveMods" badge** (Nova): an embed creators put on their pages → backlinks
  and referral traffic.
- **Biweekly community vote** (Nova drafts, operator approves rules and prize budget):
  only mods first listed in the period are eligible; a creator cannot win again for 90 days;
  votes only from established accounts (email verified, account age and activity floor), one per
  person, fraud review before announcing (a script made ~115 accounts in October — Q33). Official
  rules: eligibility, no purchase necessary, age limits (audience skews young). Prize: cash or
  gift card + homepage/newsletter/shorts feature.
