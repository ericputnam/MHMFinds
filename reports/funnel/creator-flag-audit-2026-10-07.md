## Diagnosis (Nova, for Quinn's 10-08 run)

1. **The 338 (343 today) are not a leaking supply pool.** 20 are seed placeholders and 1 is the operator. Of the 322 humans, all 322 signed up through the form and ticked "I'm a creator". They behave like players: 57.1% clicked a download vs 55.9% of unflagged form sign-ups, and 41.9% favorited vs 53.3%. 0.9% (3) submitted. The tick rate holds at 13–24% of form sign-ups every month since 2025-12, which is what a self-description checkbox does, not a creator cohort.
2. **Only 1 of 322 matches a catalog author slug** (`layla`, 14 mods, 0 favorites, 0 clicks). It is a common first name, so it is a weak lead and not an identity. 18 more have creator-sounding handles (also weak). The actual supply pool is still the catalog's authors, which means outreach (Q23), not this flag.
3. **The one structural gap this does show:** a ticked creator never gets sent to the portal. Sign-up returns to the page they came from (`resolveSignInReturn` in `app/sign-in/page.tsx`), there is no welcome email (`app/api/auth/signup/route.ts` sends nothing), and the only entry point is a navbar "Creator Portal" button. GA4: at most 18 of 145 recent flagged accounts opened `/creators/`, and 1 reached `/creators/submit/`.
4. **Recommendation:** stop citing "338 flagged / 3 submitted" as a leak, because the denominator is self-description. If we want a cheap test, route `isCreator` sign-ups to `/creators/` once (T1, `app/sign-in/page.tsx` `returnPath`, which is a held path today). The read would be `/creators/` users per flagged sign-up, from 18/145 (12.4%, 90d). Worth doing only after Q23, because the expected yield is ~1 submission per 100 flagged accounts.
