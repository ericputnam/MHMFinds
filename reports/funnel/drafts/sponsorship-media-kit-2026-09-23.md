### Rates — two options for the operator to pick from (Tier 2: the price is his call)

| Package | What's in it | Price | Effective CPM on the two on-site slots |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — Hub sponsor** | New Mods hub line + newsletter line | **$300 / month** | $47.60 on 6,300 pv (hub only) |

Both are above display-ad rates (site page RPM $10.97) on purpose: this is a single-sponsor, category-exclusive, human-written line, not a programmatic impression. Comparable Sims/CC creator sponsorships on Patreon and YouTube price at $20–60 CPM. **Floor: never below $300/month** — below that the slot is worth less than the operator's hour to run it.

### Terms (the operator's; suggested)

- Month-to-month, paid in advance (Stripe invoice or PayPal — operator's existing account, no new billing stack).
- Sponsor supplies a logo (SVG/PNG, ≤ 40 px high) and one landing URL. Copy is written by us; sponsor approves.
- Category exclusivity for the month. No adult, gambling, or crypto. No mod-piracy or paywall-bypass products.
- "Presented by" is labelled as sponsorship (FTC / ASA compliant).
- Reporting: pageviews on the sponsored pages + click-through count at month end, from GA4.

### Who to email first (categories, ranked by fit; the operator picks the names)

1. **Sims CC creators with a Patreon of ≥ 1,000 paid members** — they already spend on visibility, our audience is literally their customer, and a $300 slot is cheaper than one paid TikTok. Source of candidates: the 20 creator profiles on the site + the top-favorited creators in the catalog (`CreatorProfile`, `Mod.creator`).
2. **Mod managers / CC tools** — anything a reader installs before installing CC (mod organisers, tray importers, CC managers). Small companies with a marketing line and a direct founder inbox.
3. **PC peripherals with a gaming line** — keyboards, mice, monitors, storage. 93.6% desktop + 6-minute sessions is the pitch.
4. **Game-pass / storefront affiliates** (EA app, Steam key resellers with an affiliate program) — lower fit, but they have budgets and buy on CPM.
5. **VPN / cloud storage** — buys everywhere; last resort, keeps the category-exclusive slot from going empty.

## Measurement (rule written before anything is sent)

- **Metric:** sponsorship line of non-ad revenue (`targets.json` → `sponsorship`, today **$0**), and its leading indicators: outreach emails sent, replies, calls booked, first invoice.
- **Baseline (2026-09-23):** 0 emails sent, 0 sponsors ever, sponsorship $0/month, non-ad total $142/month public ($144.50 API).
- **Read on:** **2026-10-07** (14 days).
- **Keep / proceed:** ≥ 10 emails sent AND (≥ 1 reply-with-interest OR ≥ 1 call booked) → keep the kit, build the "presented by" slot as a Tier 1 PR behind a flag (`NEXT_PUBLIC_SPONSOR_*`) so it can go live the day a sponsor pays. ≥ 10 sent and 0 interest → rewrite the pitch around Package A only and try 10 creators (category 1) before dropping. **0 sent by 2026-09-30** → re-pitch once in the 09-30 digest, then drop and log per the 7-day rule.
- **Ad guardrail:** no site change in this experiment. When a slot ships it is copy outside every `.mv-ads`/`aside#secondary` anchor; page RPM on the two host pages is read against the E60 floors (home $8.89, blog hub $13.66, one-sided).
- **Cost of waiting:** the B-line prices "one sponsor ≈ $300" into the 10-31 non-ad target of $450/month; today's $142 cannot reach it on Patreon joins alone (0.43/day over the last 7 days → +$39/month by 10-31 at $3). Every month without a sponsor is the whole gap.
- **Silence default:** nothing is sent, nothing changes on the site; the package is re-pitched once (09-30) and dropped 10-07.

## Rollback

None needed — no site surface. If a sponsor is signed and later ends, the placement flag flips off in one env change (Tier 2, ~1 minute).
