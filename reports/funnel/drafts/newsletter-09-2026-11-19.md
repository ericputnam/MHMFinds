## What E436 fixed before this issue (same PR)
The first 11-19 dry run (before the fix, ledger row 11:41Z) gave **5 of 6 "new since the last issue" picks from one
blog post**, `/sims-4-skincare-cc/`: Dreamy Glow Essentials, Face Mask, Aesthetic Skincare, Luminous Beauty, and
Functional Skin Care Facial Mask. The sixth was Pearl Chandelier. Issue 08 had 3 of 6 from one laundry post.
There were two causes, both in the pick step:
- **The pool starved.** The builder fetched the 30 most-downloaded rows of the 14-day window and then kept only
  images on an allowed host. 23 of those 30 had creator-hosted thumbnails (Vercel Blob or TSR), so 7 were left, all from
  two posts. The full window had **226 allowed-host rows from 10 posts** (264 rows in total). The fetch is now
  `RECENT_CANDIDATE_TAKE = 200`.
- **The spread was keyed on the wrong field.** `diversifyByContentType` takes one row per content type. One
  listicle's rows carry different, often wrong, types (the skincare five were typed furniture, blush, null,
  clutter and skin), so the type rule let all five through. The new `diversifyBySourcePost` takes one row per
  `sourceUrl` first, then tops up in download order. A row with no `sourceUrl` falls back to its type key, so the old
  behaviour holds for those rows.
- **Sims 4 only.** The 28-day window has 55 Minecraft and 10 Stardew rows. A per-post spread would reach them
  once Sims posts ran short, so the adapter now asks for `gameVersion = 'Sims 4'` or null.
- The 4 new tests in `__tests__/unit/newsletter-weekly.test.ts` were run against the pre-fix builder: 4 of 4 red.
