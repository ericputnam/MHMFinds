## What E358 changed before this issue (same PR)
Before E358 the builder was still weekly in four places:
- **Number:** the weekly counter read **09** on 11-06, so the issue after 07 would have been labelled and
  UTM-tagged `weekly-issue-09`. Now **08** (`computeIssueNumber`: weekly through 07, then +1 per 14 days from 10-20).
- **Window:** the picks came from the **last 7 days**, so mods added in the first week of each two-week gap never
  got a slot. Now 14 days first (`ISSUE_WINDOW_DAYS`), widening to 21 and then 28.
- **Copy:** the subject was "This week on MustHaveMods". The intro said "this week … last 7 days". The section labels
  were "New this week" and "Most-saved this week", and the second one was never true, because that list is all-time
  favourites. All four now say two weeks / since the last issue / "Most-saved".
- **Bounce attribution:** the new subject prefix "New on MustHaveMods" was added to `CAMPAIGN_SUBJECTS`
  (`scripts/agents/_mailbox_readonly.py`), so issue-08 DSNs are counted under `biweekly` and not filed as "other" (the E254 class).
