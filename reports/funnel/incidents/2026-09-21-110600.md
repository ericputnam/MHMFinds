# Incident 2026-09-21 11:06Z — production alias twice fell behind `main` (two `vercel promote`s); no rollback, no user-facing failure

**Mode:** after-merge (Cass #132, Nova #136) · **Commits:** `84c2574` (#132), `7816cbe` (#136), `5641326` (#133), `f707130` (#135) · **Final production build:** `f707130` (main HEAD at 11:17Z), verified PASS 07:15 row

## What happened
- 11:04:55Z Rio merged #133 (docs). 11:05:00Z Cass merged #132 (homepage capture strip) — 5 s later, because the serialization re-check was chained with `gh pr merge` in one command and could not gate.
- Vercel built both. #133's build (parent of #132, no strip) went READY 11 s after #132's and took the production alias. The first `deploy-verify` for #132 (07:05 row, PASS) was measured against that build; production served no strip 11:06→11:11Z (~5 min).
- Cass caught it by curling production for the strip's heading (absent), confirmed via `vercel inspect`, and ran `vercel promote` on `84c2574`'s build. That promote put production *behind* Nova's #136 (`7816cbe`, kids-cc, merged 11:09:47Z) for ~2 min (11:14:54→11:17:09Z). Cass promoted `7816cbe`'s build; Sage's #135 build of `f707130` (main HEAD) then took the alias at 11:17:09Z.
- Nova's first verify (07:09 row) likewise graded a build that did not contain `7816cbe`; the 07:17 recheck row is the true one.

## Impact
- No 5xx, ad anchors and blog markers passed on every check. Visitors briefly saw the previous homepage (no capture strip) and, for ~2 min, no `/games/sims-4/kids-cc/` page. Nothing rolled back; no incident action by the runner.

## Root cause
- Two merges 5 s apart → Vercel coalesced/overlapping builds; the alias goes to whichever build finishes last, not to `main` HEAD. `deploy-verify.sh --after-merge` verifies "the caller's sha is READY" and does not check that production's alias points at a build of `origin/main` HEAD.

## Fix (Tier 0, queued in ideas-inbox for 09-22)
1. `deploy-verify.sh --after-merge`: after the wait, compare the alias's build sha to `origin/main` HEAD; if main has moved past the caller's sha, verify and (if needed) promote the newest READY build of HEAD — never the caller's sha.
2. The merge-serialization check becomes a standalone command that exits non-zero when the newest merge is < 4 min old, so it can gate.
